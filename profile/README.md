Black Canyon Software builds two things: its own products, and workflow
automation for small businesses still doing the work by hand.

The repositories here are private. This page is the index.

## Products

**Vantage** — tells a game studio where its players need help. Players who get
stuck rarely post about it; they search. Vantage reads the questions they ask
search engines, groups them into the problems behind them, and ranks those
problems week over week against the game's own patch history. Three seeds
against Elden Ring surfaced *"why is everything hostile"* and *"what is poise"*
on the first run. Milestone 0: the pipeline runs end to end on one game.

**Market Predictor** — cross-sectional equity return forecasting across the
S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100, ranking the universe by expected excess return over
one, three and five trading days rather than guessing at price. The backtest is
complete and the pipeline runs unattended; the out-of-sample record is still
filling, and nothing is claimed until it is in.

**ai-suite** — a complete local AI stack on one machine with no API spend. One
OpenAI-compatible endpoint fronts every model, and an orchestrator delegates to
specialists for coding, image generation, speech and research, loading each on
demand.

**doctools** — one command that turns PDF, Word, PowerPoint and the rest into
clean text a language model can actually read: equations as LaTeX, diagrams as
cropped images, boilerplate discarded, and an exact report of what was removed.

**Pixel Galaxy Studio** — a local web app for designing a pixel-art galaxy
background and exporting it as a seamlessly looping MP4, for Wallpaper Engine,
a phone lock screen, or anywhere a looping video works. Every knob on a panel,
a full-size live preview, and a frame-exact encoder.

**DAYnNIGHT** — a live Windows desktop wallpaper that is the sky outside right
now. Real solar and lunar positions, real twilight, tonight's moon phase and
four thousand stars turning at the sidereal rate, computed from your clock and
your coordinates rather than played back from a video. A full day costs a few
hundred kilobytes of shader instead of tens of gigabytes of frames, and 0.15%
of one CPU core to leave running.

## Automation for small business

**Quote-It** reads the quote requests arriving in a contractor's inbox and
turns them into draft estimates, flagging the ones a human should look at and
ignoring the ones that are not requests at all.

In the demo, sixteen emails from a fictional HVAC contractor's inbox become
eleven draft estimates, two items in a review queue, and three correctly
ignored — with no API key, no network and no cost. One codebase serves every
client; there is never a fork per customer.

If you spend hours a week retyping the same estimates, that is the conversation
to have.
